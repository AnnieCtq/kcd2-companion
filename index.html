A guide for new players

This companion keeps the records so the table can keep playing. One person runs it on a phone or laptop for the whole party.

1. Gather the party

Enter two to four names and a colour for each. Everyone starts with 1,000 Groschen unless you change it. Press Begin Chapter I.

2. Shared story, personal Glory

The two boxes at the top are kept apart on purpose.

  • Campaign status belongs to the whole party. Tap the next chapter when the party advances.
  • Glory belongs to each player. The crown ♛ marks the current leader.

We win the main story together, but the player with the most Glory becomes Champion of Bohemia if the party succeeds.

3. During play

  • Clues: when a quest lead pays off, record the clue and who found it. The finder gets +1 Glory automatically, and the clue joins the party's shared evidence.
  • Glory: award it for a significant quest contribution, a tournament win or a character achievement. Never for rent or buying property.
  • Treasury: log rent (who paid whom), event money gained or lost, quest rewards and purchases. Balances update on their own.

The Campaign tab has the clue and Glory forms side by side for quick use mid-turn.

4. The finale

  1. On the Finale tab, tick unspent clues to add +1 each to the final check. All bonuses together stop at +2, and a spent clue is gone for good.
  2. Declare the party's fate: success or unresolved. Announce this first.
  3. Only if the party succeeds, the Champion of Bohemia is revealed.

5. Mistakes and saving

  • ↶ Undo (next to the tabs) reverses the last action. Small ✕ buttons remove single entries.
  • Everything is saved in this browser. Use Save chronicle at the bottom to keep a copy or move the game to another device, and Load chronicle to bring it back.
  • After the game, the Finale tab can copy a summary or download the money ledger for your playtest notes.
